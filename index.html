Projects

One app in active development, one research project in the design stage, and one design concept. Only Angel Wing is being built.

Concept · Accessibility · Teens

Rannaghor — an accessible cooking companion

A recipe app for women with physical disabilities and for children and teens cooking with supervision: large touch targets, voice and one-tap control, seated and one-hand adaptations, and a kid-safe mode that flags hot or sharp steps for an adult.

Methods: interviews, contextual inquiry, Figma prototype, usability tests

In progress · Mobile app · Plants

Angel Wing — a houseplant care app

A local-first iOS and Android app (Expo, React Native, TypeScript, SQLite) that estimates watering, fertilizing and repotting intervals from the daily light integral each plant receives from windows and grow lamps, and corrects them from soil-check feedback. It has a tested care engine, an offline database, daily reminders and JSON backup, with a dataset of 60 species and 15 lamps. Not released yet.

Stack: Expo, React Native, TypeScript, SQLite, Vitest

Research design · Privacy · Young adults

Daily Light — a privacy-first sharing app for women and girls

Phase 1 (planned user study): why do young adults feel unsafe sharing photos and videos on Instagram, Snapchat, Facebook and Threads, and would a private space for women and girls only, with screenshot protection and alerts, make sharing feel safer?

Phase 2: how to build such privacy-preserving features, comparing on-device processing with cloud approaches.

Status: research design stage. No study has been run and nothing is built yet. A study with human participants needs ethics approval.

Research direction

I am interested in how everyday-activity technologies can support the wellbeing of women of different ages, incomes, abilities and mental-health conditions, using qualitative and co-design methods.
