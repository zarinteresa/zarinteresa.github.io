# R&D project concepts (draft)

All three are **concepts — not built**. Status is shown as such on the website. Shared goal: wellbeing tools for women/girls of different ages, classes and abilities (see `research-motivation.md`).

## 1. Rannaghor — an accessible cooking companion
- **Users:** women with physical/motor disabilities; children and teens cooking with supervision.
- **Idea:** step-by-step recipes with large targets, voice/one-tap control, "one-hand" and "seated" adaptations, kid-safe mode (hot/sharp steps flagged for an adult), adaptive-tool checklist.
- **Research Qs:** what blocks cooking independence? what does safe confidence look like for teens?
- **Methods:** interviews + contextual inquiry, Figma prototype, usability tests.
- **Related work:** accessible-cooking studies exist mainly for visual impairment — motor/teen angle is the gap.

## 2. Sprout — plant care for absolute beginners
- **Users:** first-time plant owners, incl. women managing stress; low budget.
- **Idea:** "can't-fail" starter plants, plain-language care reminders, no streak-guilt, a short mood check-in, offline-friendly, local-plant database.
- **Research Qs:** does a gentle, low-pressure plant routine support wellbeing? what makes beginners quit?
- **Methods:** diary study, small pilot, interviews.
- **Related work:** gardening pilot RCTs in women; Thrive app evaluation; no app trials for beginners found.

## 3. Daily Light — a private-first daily vlog/diary
- **Users:** teen girls and women who want to reflect without public exposure, incl. those with anxiety/depression.
- **Idea:** record short audio/video/text entries stored locally, optional share with one trusted person, gentle prompts, no likes/comments, easy delete.
- **Research Qs:** how does stigma shape self-recording? what privacy controls do users need at home?
- **Methods:** co-design workshops, trauma-informed interviews (needs ethics approval).
- **Related work:** teen vlog identity work; menstrual-pain vlogs; UWE co-design with girls.
